# Product Vision

## Vision

Make every enterprise network change explainable, reviewable, authorized, verifiable, and auditable.

## Product position

NCCP is an enterprise network change control plane. It is not an automation engine, a device controller, or a vendor management console.

## Primary users

- Network engineers define and review intent.
- Change managers govern lifecycle and risk.
- Security and compliance teams define policy and inspect evidence.
- Platform engineers operate integrations and execution engines.
- Auditors trace decisions and outcomes.

## Product boundaries

NCCP owns:

- change intent;
- governance state;
- policy decisions;
- render and review artifacts;
- approvals and execution authorization;
- evidence, audit, and reconciliation.

NCCP delegates:

- device-specific execution;
- vendor protocol handling;
- secret storage;
- external ticketing workflows;
- certificate authority operations.
